# The Quest for El Dorado — Base Game Cards & Effects

**Scope:** Base game only (2017 English edition). This inventory covers the 21 distinct card faces: 3 starting-card types and 18 marketplace-card types. The marketplace contains 3 copies of each type (54 cards total); each player starts with an 8-card deck.

## How to read the card details

- **Power** is the movement strength and colour/symbol shown on a card.
- **Cost** is the number of coins required to buy a marketplace card.
- **One-use item** cards are removed from the game after using their special effect. If played without using that effect (for example, as a card for rubble or as half a coin), they go to the discard pile instead.
- A normal movement card is played for one movement. Its power can carry across adjacent spaces of the same terrain, with the required power deducted as you move.
- **Green** = machete/jungle; **blue** = paddle/river; **yellow** = coin/village; **white** = choose one of the three movement symbols.

## General rules that affect card use

1. You may play any number of cards on your turn, using them to move and/or to buy **up to one** marketplace card.
2. For a normal landscape space, one card must match the space's colour/symbol and meet its power requirement. You cannot combine multiple cards to meet one space's power requirement.
3. Leftover power may be used to continue through adjacent spaces of the same terrain; subtract the power spent at each space.
4. To enter rubble or a base camp, discard a number of cards equal to the number of symbols shown. The identities and colours of those cards do not matter. Cards used to enter a base camp are removed from the game; cards used for rubble go to the discard pile.
5. Mountains and spaces occupied by any playing piece cannot be entered.
6. For buying, cards with a coin symbol contribute their printed coin value; cards without one contribute half a coin each. You may combine cards to afford one purchase. The purchased card goes to your discard pile and cannot be used that turn.
7. At the end of the turn, discard played cards that remain in play, then draw back up to 4 cards.
